Artanova Cafe – Customer & Owner Dashboard

Artanova Cafe is a responsive cafe management web application that integrates a customer ordering interface with an interactive owner dashboard. The platform allows customers to explore cafe products, place orders, and book art studio or VR gaming experiences, while cafe owners can monitor business performance, track inventory, and analyze revenue through a centralized dashboard.

✨ Key Features

🛍️ Customer Dashboard

Interactive cafe menu with beverages and pastries.

Add-to-cart functionality with quantity management.

Art studio booking with multiple session durations.

VR gaming experience selection and booking.

Customer details and order type selection.

Checkout with automatic tax calculation.

📊 Owner Dashboard

Revenue tracking with daily, monthly, and all-time filters.

Total orders and unique customer tracking.

Average Order Value (AOV) calculation.

Total items sold and sales performance monitoring.

Revenue trend visualization.

Category-wise revenue analysis.

Recent customer orders overview.

Customer feedback and satisfaction metrics.

Studio and VR booking occupancy monitoring.

📦 Smart Inventory Management

Automatic inventory deduction based on customer orders.

Ingredient-level consumption tracking.

Live stock availability monitoring.

Low-stock alerts and reorder indicators.

Manual inventory restocking functionality.

🔄 Customer-to-Owner Integration

The application demonstrates a connected ordering workflow:

Customers place orders through the customer interface.

Order details and quantities are recorded.

Inventory is automatically updated based on predefined product recipes.

Revenue, customer count, order count, and sales KPIs are recalculated.

The owner dashboard reflects the updated business performance.

🛠️ Tech Stack

HTML5 – Website structure

CSS3 – Responsive UI and styling

JavaScript – Application logic and dynamic updates

Chart.js – Revenue and sales visualizations

LocalStorage – Browser-based data persistence

📱 Responsive Design

The application is designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens, providing an accessible experience for both customers and cafe owners.
